## Protein per serving

Values are from USDA FoodData Central, sorted by protein per typical serving.

| Food | Serving | Protein per serving | Protein per 100 g |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tempeh | 1 cup (166 g) | 33.7 g | 20.3 g |
| Firm tofu (calcium sulfate) | ½ cup (126 g) | 21.8 g | 17.3 g |
| Edamame, prepared | 1 cup (155 g) | 18.5 g | 11.9 g |
| Lentils, boiled | 1 cup (198 g) | 17.9 g | 9.0 g |
| Plain nonfat Greek yogurt | 170 g pot | 17.3 g | 10.2 g |
| Black beans, boiled | 1 cup (172 g) | 15.2 g | 8.9 g |
| Chickpeas, boiled | 1 cup (164 g) | 14.5 g | 8.9 g |
| Cottage cheese, 2% | 4 oz (113 g) | 11.8 g | 10.5 g |
| Milk, 2% | 1 cup (244 g) | 8.1 g | 3.3 g |
| Peanut butter, smooth | 2 tbsp (32 g) | 7.1 g | 22.2 g |
| Egg, hard-boiled | 1 large (50 g) | 6.3 g | 12.6 g |

Peanut butter is high per 100 g but low per serving, because a serving is small and most of its calories come from fat.

## How much protein do vegetarians need?

The same as anyone else. The US Dietary Guidelines for 2025 to 2030 set a goal of 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg of body weight a day; for building muscle, aim nearer 1.6 g per kg. Use our [protein calculator](/tools/protein-calculator) for your number.

In large studies such as EPIC-Oxford and NutriNet-Santé, lacto-ovo vegetarians got about 14% of their calories from protein, roughly 1 g per kg a day. That's comfortably above the long-standing RDA of 0.8 g per kg, but below the newer guideline range, so most vegetarians who train will want to add protein on purpose.

## Do you need to combine proteins?

No. A 2019 review in *Nutrients* found no evidence that complementary plant proteins, such as beans and grains, must be eaten in the same meal. Eating a variety of protein foods across the day covers your amino acids. It also found that plant and animal proteins differ in digestibility by only a few percent.

The Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics' position is that well-planned vegetarian diets are nutritionally adequate at all life stages, including for athletes.

## A sample high-protein vegetarian day

Using only the foods in the table:

| Meal | Foods | Protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 3 eggs and a cup of 2% milk | 27 g |
| Lunch | 1 cup chickpeas and ½ cup firm tofu | 36 g |
| Snack | 170 g pot of plain Greek yogurt | 17 g |
| Dinner | 1 cup tempeh | 34 g |
| **Total** | | **114 g** |

Grains, vegetables and nuts in real meals add more on top. For an 82 kg (180 lb) person, 114 g is about 1.4 g per kg.
