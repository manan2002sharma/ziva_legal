## What the guidelines use

The 2013 American Heart Association, American College of Cardiology and The Obesity Society guideline describes three common approaches:

- A daily deficit of **500 to 750 kcal**.
- A fixed intake, typically **1,200 to 1,500 kcal a day for women** and **1,500 to 1,800 for men**.
- Very-low-calorie diets of **under 800 kcal a day**, only in a medical setting with supervision.

The CDC says people who lose about 1 to 2 lb a week are more likely to keep the weight off.

## Step 1: find your maintenance calories

Your deficit is measured from what you burn now. Our [macro calculator](/tools/macro-calculator) estimates it with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and an activity factor. For a 30-year-old man who is 5 ft 10 in, weighs 180 lb and exercises moderately, maintenance is about 2,760 kcal a day; a 500 kcal deficit gives a target of about 2,260.

For a personal projection over time, the NIH's Body Weight Planner models how your weight responds to a calorie and activity plan.

## Step 2: expect the slowdown

The familiar rule says a 3,500 kcal deficit equals 1 lb of fat, so 500 kcal a day should mean 1 lb a week, or about 52 lb a year. Real results fall short:

- In a 2013 analysis of 7 tightly supervised studies (103 adults, average deficit 1,439 kcal a day for about 65 days), people lost 20.1 lb against the 27.6 lb the rule predicted.
- The rule's mistake is treating the deficit as fixed. As you lose weight you burn less, so the same intake becomes a smaller deficit.

NIH researcher Kevin Hall's team offers a better rule of thumb for adults with overweight: each 10 kcal a day you cut changes body weight by about 1 lb eventually, with about half of the change in the first year and 95% within about three years. On that rule, a steady 500 kcal a day cut leads to about 50 lb eventually, roughly 25 lb of it in the first year, half what the 3,500-calorie rule suggests.

## Step 3: protect muscle while you diet

Keep protein high and keep lifting. In one trial, men in a deficit who ate 2.4 g of protein per kg a day gained lean mass while losing fat. See [losing fat and building muscle at the same time](/blog/how-to-lose-fat-and-build-muscle-at-the-same-time).

## When to get help first

If you're pregnant, under 18, have a health condition or a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before cutting calories.
