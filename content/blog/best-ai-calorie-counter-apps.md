## What an AI calorie counter does

An AI calorie counter logs a meal from a photo, and often from a sentence or a voice note, then estimates its calories, protein, carbs and fat. The differences are in what comes with it: a free tier, a barcode scanner for packaged food, targets that adjust, or a plan to eat to.

## Cal AI: the best-known photo counter

Cal AI turns a photo, a barcode or a description into calories and macros, and its website says it estimates food volume with the phone's depth sensor. Its App Store listing shows a 4.8 rating from about 375,000 ratings. Food scanning results need a subscription, with a 3-day free trial; the App Store lists "Unlimited" plans from $2.99 to $29.99.

## SnapCalorie: built around portion size

SnapCalorie says it measures portions with the iPhone's camera and depth sensors, then pulls nutrition from the USDA database, covering more than 100 nutrients. It logs from a photo or a voice note. Premium costs $19.99 a month or $149 a year.

## MacroFactor: photo logging plus adaptive coaching

MacroFactor logs by AI photo, description, voice, barcode or nutrition label, and adjusts your calorie and macro targets each week from your logs and weight. No free tier; $11.99 a month or $71.99 a year after a free trial.

## Lose It!, MyFitnessPal and Cronometer: free trackers with paid AI logging

All three let you log by search for free and charge for photo logging. Lose It! puts photo, voice and barcode logging in Premium. MyFitnessPal puts meal scan, voice and barcode in Premium at $79.99 a year. Cronometer keeps barcode scanning free and puts photo and voice logging in Gold at $59.99 a year.

## Lifesum, Foodvisor and BitePal: logging with extras

Lifesum logs by photo, voice, text or barcode and includes meal plans with grocery lists. Foodvisor logs by photo, voice, search or barcode, and its Premium adds micronutrient analysis, a nutrition programme, workout sessions and fasting programmes. BitePal scans meals from a photo, gives a nutrition score and turns tracking into a game with a raccoon you dress up.

## ZivaAI: photo logging next to a plan

ZivaAI plans a week of meals with calories and macros for each, plus workouts and a grocery list. Tick a planned meal and its numbers are logged; photograph or describe anything else and Ziva estimates it, with what it assumed shown on the card. It also logs your sets and keeps daily progress photos.

## How to choose

- **Just photograph and go:** Cal AI.
- **Portion size matters most:** SnapCalorie.
- **Targets that adjust each week:** MacroFactor.
- **Start free, pay for photo logging later:** Lose It!, MyFitnessPal or Cronometer.
- **Meal plans included:** Lifesum, or ZivaAI if you want workouts planned too.
