## Choose Fitbod if

You want a mature strength planner that adapts every session to your recovery and progress, on more platforms including Android and Wear OS.

## Choose Planfit if

You want to start free, or you like asking an AI coach questions mid-week. Premium unlocks the coach, recovery tracking and Apple Watch support.
