## The short version

These apps solve different problems. Fitbod answers "what should I lift today?" better than anything else we looked at. ZivaAI answers "what should my whole week look like?", meaning meals, training and the shopping for them.

## Choose Fitbod if

Strength progress is the goal and you log every set. Fitbod's sessions adjust to your recovery and progress, it has a much bigger video library, and it runs on Android and Apple Watch. It's also cheaper.

## Choose ZivaAI if

You want the whole week decided: meals that fit your calorie target and diet, workouts on the days you choose, and one grocery list. You also log what you eat from a photo and tick off your sets in the same app. Because it's one plan, a change carries through. Ask Ziva to add a rest day or swap a dinner and the plan, groceries and reminders update together.

ZivaAI is {{availability}}, with no free trial.

## Using both

Some people will want Fitbod for lifting and ZivaAI for food and the rest of the week. That works, but you'd pay for both; check whether ZivaAI's workouts are enough for you first.
