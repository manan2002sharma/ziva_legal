## The prompt that gets a usable plan

ChatGPT's plan is only as good as what you tell it. Give it the same details a dietitian would ask for. Copy this and fill in the brackets:

> Make me a 7-day meal plan. I'm [age], [sex], [height], [weight], and my goal is to [lose weight / keep my weight / build muscle]. Aim for about [calories] kcal and [protein] g of protein a day. I eat [diet, e.g. vegetarian or halal] and I'm allergic to [allergies]. I don't like [foods]. I like [cuisines]. I can cook for [minutes] on weeknights and my grocery budget is [budget]. Give breakfast, lunch, dinner and one snack each day, with calories and protein for every meal and grams for each ingredient. Reuse ingredients across the week to cut waste. Then give me one grocery list for the week grouped by aisle.

If you don't know your calories or protein, work them out first with our [macro calculator](/tools/macro-calculator) and [protein calculator](/tools/protein-calculator).

## Where ChatGPT does well

- **It's flexible.** It can follow unusual diets, use what's in your fridge or plan around a busy Thursday.
- **It explains.** Ask why a meal fits your goal, or for a lower-calorie swap, and you get an answer straight away.
- **It's free to start.** ChatGPT Plus costs $20 a month if you need more.

## Where a general chatbot falls short

These are practical limits of using a chat thread as a meal planner.

- **The plan lives in a conversation.** There's no calendar view of the week, no checking off meals, and changes mean scrolling back through the chat.
- **Numbers are estimates.** Calories and macros come from the model's answer. Check the important ones, especially if you're on a tight target.
- **Changes don't flow through.** Swap Wednesday's dinner and you have to ask again for an updated grocery list.
- **Reminders need Plus.** Scheduled tasks aren't in the free plan.
- **Workouts are separate.** You can ask for a training plan too, but it won't stay coordinated with your meals unless you keep both in the same thread.

## When an app is worth paying for

If one plan a month is enough, ChatGPT with a good prompt will do. A meal planner app is worth it when you want the plan to persist and stay organized: a week you can see at a glance, nutrition attached to every meal, a grocery list that updates when a meal changes, and reminders.
