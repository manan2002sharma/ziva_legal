## What MacroFactor does

MacroFactor is a macro tracker with an adaptive coach. It estimates how many calories you actually burn from your own logs and weight, then adjusts your calorie and macro targets at weekly check-ins, in Coached, Collaborative or Manual mode. Logging is by AI photo, description, voice, barcode or nutrition label, from a verified database of more than 1.36 million foods. There's a free trial but no free tier: $11.99 a month, $47.99 for six months or $71.99 a year. Its workout app is separate, and the bundle costs $89.99 a year.

## Carbon: the other adaptive coach

Carbon Diet Coach sets calorie, protein, carb and fat targets from your answers, then checks in weekly. It looks at your multi-week weight trend and what you logged, adjusts your macros or holds them, and explains the decision in a check-in report. It logs from a photo or description matched to a verified food database, a barcode or a nutrition label. It sets protein from your lean body mass, supports carb cycling and fasting, and has a calorie planner you can match to what you ate. It costs $11.99 a month, $59.99 for six months or $99.99 a year.

**Best for:** people who want adaptive coaching and like to see the reasoning behind each change.

## Cronometer: detailed tracking for less

Cronometer has a curated database of more than 1.1 million verified foods and tracks more than 95 vitamins and minerals. Barcode scanning is free; Gold adds photo and voice logging for $10.99 a month or $59.99 a year. It doesn't adjust your targets for you.

**Best for:** people who want accurate data and are happy to set their own targets.

## MyFitnessPal and Lose It!: free logging

Both have free food logging and big databases. MyFitnessPal charges for barcode scanning, meal scan and voice logging with Premium at $79.99 a year; Lose It! keeps photo, voice and barcode logging in Premium. Neither adjusts targets from your weight trend. See [MyFitnessPal vs MacroFactor](/blog/myfitnesspal-vs-macrofactor) for the head-to-head.

## Cal AI: faster logging, less coaching

Cal AI is built for photo logging: snap a meal, scan a barcode or describe it. It sets targets from your answers but doesn't describe weekly adaptive check-ins. "Unlimited" plans on the App Store range from $2.99 to $29.99.

## ZivaAI: a plan instead of a tracker

ZivaAI doesn't adjust targets from your logs. It writes a week of meals to your calorie and macro targets, with recipes, plus workouts and one grocery list, and changes them when you ask Ziva. You can log meals from a photo or a sentence and log your sets, but Ziva doesn't read those logs to change the plan.
