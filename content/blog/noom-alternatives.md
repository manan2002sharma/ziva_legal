## What Noom gives you

Noom Weight is a behavior-change program. Its app lists meal plans and dietitian-backed recipes, Noom Move with more than 1,000 fitness, stretching and meditation classes, AI meal logging, and optional one-to-one coaching from human coaches. An annual plan costs $209, or about $17.42 a month. Noom's medical programs, including GLP-1 prescriptions, are separate and cost more.

## MacroFactor: track and let the targets adjust

MacroFactor adjusts your calorie and macro targets each week from what you log, with barcode, label and AI photo logging. It costs $11.99 a month or $71.99 a year after a 7-day trial, and its workout app is a separate subscription.

## Eat This Much: let the app decide what you eat

Eat This Much writes your meals automatically to your calorie target and emails a weekly grocery list with Premium. It's the cheapest way to get a structured meal plan, at $59.99 a year.

## MyFitnessPal: free logging, paid planning

Food and exercise logging is free. The meal planner is in Premium+, at $24.99 a month or $99.99 a year.

## ZivaAI: a plan for meals and training

ZivaAI writes a week of meals with recipes and macros, workouts for your equipment and a grocery list, and you change any of it by chatting with Ziva. You log meals from a photo or a sentence and tick off your sets, and a daily progress photo builds into a timelapse. It has no human coaches and no psychology lessons, so it isn't a like-for-like Noom replacement. ## Which should you choose?

- **Lowest cost:** Eat This Much or MacroFactor.
- **A human checking in on you:** stay with Noom, or look at a coach-led app.
- **Meals and workouts planned for you, changeable by chat:** ZivaAI.
