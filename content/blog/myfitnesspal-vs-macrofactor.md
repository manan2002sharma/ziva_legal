## Choose MyFitnessPal if

You want free logging, a food database of more than 20 million foods, or the meal planner in Premium+.

## Choose MacroFactor if

You'll log consistently and want your calorie and macro targets to adjust to your real progress. It's cheaper per year than MyFitnessPal Premium.
