## What MyFitnessPal gives you

MyFitnessPal is the largest food diary, with about 2.4 million App Store ratings. Food and exercise logging is free. Premium adds barcode scanning, meal scan and voice logging, custom macro goals and ad-free logging, for $19.99 a month or $79.99 a year. Premium+ adds meal plans and grocery delivery for $24.99 a month or $99.99 a year. It also has an AI Coach that answers questions about your logs.

## Lose It!: the closest like-for-like swap

Lose It! works the same way as MyFitnessPal: a calorie budget, free logging by search from more than 56 million foods and recipes, and a Premium tier. Premium adds photo and AI voice logging, the barcode scanner, macro and nutrient tracking, fasting and meal targets. Its App Store listing shows several Premium prices, from $9.99 to $79.99.

**Best for:** people who want MyFitnessPal's approach with a different app.

## Cronometer: accuracy over size

Cronometer uses a curated database of more than 1.1 million verified foods and tracks more than 95 vitamins and minerals. Barcode scanning is free, which MyFitnessPal and Lose It! both charge for. Photo and voice logging come with Gold, at $10.99 a month or $59.99 a year.

**Best for:** people who want reliable entries and micronutrients.

## MacroFactor: targets that change with you

MacroFactor estimates how many calories you burn from your logs and weight, then adjusts your targets at weekly check-ins. It logs by AI photo, description, voice, barcode and nutrition label. There's no free tier; it costs $11.99 a month or $71.99 a year after a free trial. For a full comparison, see [MyFitnessPal vs MacroFactor](/blog/myfitnesspal-vs-macrofactor).

## Cal AI: logging from photos

Cal AI is built around the camera: photograph a meal, scan a barcode or describe it. It has a 3-day free trial and runs on iPhone, Apple Watch and Android. Its App Store listing shows "Unlimited" plans from $2.99 to $29.99.

## Lifesum: tracking plus meal plans

Lifesum logs by photo, voice, text or barcode, and includes meal plans with grocery lists, from balanced plans to keto, paleo and high-protein. It's free to use with limited features; Premium costs $99.99 to $119.99 a year on the App Store.

## Yazio: a low-cost tracker with fasting

Yazio combines a free calorie counter with a barcode scanner, intermittent fasting and meal planning tools. YAZIO PRO costs $23.90 to $47.90 for 12 months on the App Store.

## ZivaAI: a plan first, logging beside it

ZivaAI works the other way round from MyFitnessPal. It writes a week of meals with calories and macros, plus workouts and a grocery list, and you change the plan by chatting with Ziva. To log, tick a planned meal, or photograph or describe anything else for an estimate. It also logs your sets.

## Which should you choose?

- **The same thing, free, elsewhere:** Lose It!.
- **Accurate entries and micronutrients, with a free barcode scanner:** Cronometer.
- **Targets that adjust each week:** MacroFactor.
- **Log by photo:** Cal AI.
- **Meal plans included:** Lifesum, or ZivaAI if you want workouts planned too.
- **A low-cost yearly plan with fasting:** Yazio.
