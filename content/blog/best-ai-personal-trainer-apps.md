## What an AI personal trainer app does

It replaces the planning part of a trainer: which exercises to do today, how many sets and reps, and how to progress. The good ones build around your equipment and history; some add a chat coach for questions. None can watch your form in the room.

## Fitbod: the established strength planner

Fitbod reads your level, goals and equipment, picks each day's workout and updates it as you recover and progress, with more than 1,000 exercise videos. It's on iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, Vision Pro and Android.

## Planfit: free planner, AI coach in Premium

Planfit builds plans around your strength, schedule and gym equipment, free. Premium adds a real-time AI coach, recovery tracking and Apple Watch support.

## Freeletics: bodyweight HIIT, weights and running

Freeletics' AI coach adjusts each session from your feedback. It's sold in 3, 6 and 12-month plans, with nutrition available as a bundle.

## ZivaAI: training planned with your meals

ZivaAI plans sessions of 20 to 60 minutes for a full gym, a few dumbbells or no equipment, around a sore knee, shoulder or back, with pictures and steps for each exercise. It plans your meals and grocery list in the same plan, and you can ask Ziva to move a workout or swap an exercise. You log sets as you go, from the app or the Lock Screen, with last session's numbers beside each exercise and your personal bests saved, and you can log meals from a photo.

## When a human coach is worth it

If you need someone to hold you accountable or check your form, Ladder's coach-led teams start at $29.99 a month, and Future's one-to-one coaching costs $199 a month.
