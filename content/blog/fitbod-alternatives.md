## What Fitbod does

Fitbod builds strength and endurance workouts from your level, goals and equipment, and updates them with your recovery and progress. It has more than 1,000 exercise videos and runs on iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, Vision Pro and Android. The App Store lists monthly plans at $12.99 and $15.99 and yearly plans at $79.99 and $95.99. It doesn't cover nutrition, and trainers answer questions by email rather than chat.

## Planfit: closest match, with a free tier

Planfit builds AI workout plans around your strength, goals, schedule and gym equipment, and the planner and log are free. Premium adds a real-time AI coach, recovery tracking and Apple Watch support.

## Alpha Progression: set-by-set progression for muscle growth

Alpha Progression's Pro tier builds a plan from your goal, training days, session length, priority muscles and gym equipment, then shows the weight and reps to aim for before every set. Logging is free with no account, Pro has a 14-day free trial, and its App Store listing shows $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year.

## Boostcamp: proven programs, free

Boostcamp is a free tracker with more than 11,000 programs, including well-known routines such as nSuns, GZCLP, 5/3/1 and Reddit PPL, plus coach-written programs and an AI coach that builds a plan from your goal, schedule and equipment. Its App Store listing shows several Pro prices, from $11.99 to $79.99.

## Hevy: the free logger

Hevy is a free, ad-free workout log for any program or split, with exercise videos and a social feed. Hevy Pro adds Hevy Trainer, which builds a program from your experience, goal and equipment; Pro costs $2.99 to $3.99 a month, $23.99 a year or $74.99 for life.

## Jefit: for people who like programs and data

Jefit combines a workout tracker, ready-made programs, exercise demos and estimated one-rep maxes, with a free Basic tier. Elite costs $69.99 a year.

## Freeletics: bodyweight, weights and running

Freeletics' AI Training Coach adjusts each workout from your feedback and covers bodyweight HIIT, weights and running. There's no monthly plan; subscriptions run 3, 6 or 12 months, with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

## Ladder and Future: human coaches

Ladder puts you on a coach-led team with a new strength workout each day. Future pairs you with a personal coach who writes and adjusts your program, at $199 a month. Choose these if accountability from a person matters more than price.

## ZivaAI: workouts planned with your meals

ZivaAI plans sessions of 20 to 60 minutes for a full gym, a few dumbbells or no equipment, around any sore knee, shoulder or back, with pictures and steps for each of 601 exercises. Unlike every app above, it plans your meals and grocery list in the same plan, and you change any of it by chatting with Ziva. Sets are logged as you go, from the app or the Lock Screen, and meals from a photo.
