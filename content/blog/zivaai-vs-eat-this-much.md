## Side by side

| | ZivaAI | Eat This Much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builds meal plans | Yes, a full week from a few questions | Yes; weekly with Premium, one day at a time free |
| Inputs | Goal, diet, allergies, foods you avoid, 12 cuisines, cooking skill, grocery budget | Calorie and macro goals, preferred foods, budget, schedule |
| Grocery list | Yes, sorted by aisle | Yes, emailed with the weekly plan (Premium) |
| Food logging | Yes: tick a planned meal, or get an estimate from a meal photo or a sentence | Yes, free; reads nutrition labels from photos |
| Workouts | Yes, planned with your meals, and logged set by set | No |
| Change the plan | Ask Ziva in chat; see the change first, then accept or undo | Edit in the app |
| Platforms | iPhone and iPad | iPhone and iPad (check its site for others) |
| Free option | No | One-day plans; 14-day Premium trial |
| US price | {{price_monthly}}/month or {{price_yearly}}/year | $14.99/month or $59.99/year |

## Choose Eat This Much if

You want automatic meal plans at the lowest price, you want to log food for free, or you want to try before you pay. At $59.99 a year it costs less than a quarter of ZivaAI's yearly price.

## Choose ZivaAI if

You want your training planned and logged in the same app as your meals, you'd rather photograph a meal than look it up, or you'd rather ask for changes in plain words than edit settings. "Make Thursday lunch vegetarian" or "make dinners quicker this week" changes the meals, the grocery list and your reminders together.
