## Count sets per muscle per week

The most useful number is weekly sets per muscle group. In 2026 the American College of Sports Medicine updated its resistance training guidance from a review of 137 systematic reviews. For muscle growth it recommends higher weekly volume, about **10 sets per muscle group**, with every major muscle trained at least twice a week.

That matches a 2017 meta-analysis by Schoenfeld and colleagues, which found a graded dose-response: each extra weekly set was associated with about 0.37% more muscle growth.

## Reps and load

| Goal | Load | Reps and sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muscle growth | Moderate | Mostly 6 to 12 reps (ACSM 2009), about 10+ sets per muscle a week (ACSM 2026) |
| Strength | Heavy, about 80% of one-rep max | 2 to 3 sets per exercise (ACSM 2026) |
| Power | 30 to 70% of one-rep max, moved fast | Per ACSM 2026 |

Light weights work too. A 2017 meta-analysis of 21 studies compared loads of 60% of one-rep max or less with heavier loads, with every set taken to failure. Muscle growth was similar; strength gains were greater with heavy loads. So with lighter weights, take sets close to failure.

## Rest between sets

ACSM's 2009 position stand suggested 1 to 2 minutes between sets for muscle growth.

## When to add weight

ACSM's 2009 rule: once you can do 1 or 2 reps more than your target on two consecutive sessions, add 2 to 10% to the load.

## Putting it into a week

Ten sets per muscle a week can come from two sessions of five sets, or four shorter sessions. Here's a four-day layout where sessions A and B each run twice:

| Muscle group | Session A | Session B | Sets in A + B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chest | Bench press 3 × 8–10 | Incline dumbbell press 3 × 10–12 | 6 |
| Back | Row 3 × 10 | Lat pulldown 3 × 10–12 | 6 |
| Quads | Squat 3 × 8 | Split squat 2 × 10 each leg | 5 |
| Hamstrings | Romanian deadlift 3 × 8 | Leg curl 2 × 12 | 5 |

Run A and B twice each a week, four sessions in all, and every row doubles: 10 to 12 sets per muscle. See [full body vs split workouts](/blog/full-body-vs-split-workout) for other layouts.
