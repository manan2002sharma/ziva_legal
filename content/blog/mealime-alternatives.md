## What happened to Mealime

Mealime's website now carries a notice that the app "will shut down on October 21, 2026." In the run-up, its App Store notes said Pro features were free for everyone and existing subscriptions would not renew.

Mealime was popular for one simple job: pick a few quick recipes, get a weekly plan, and get a grocery list sorted for the store. Pro cost $2.99 a month. Any replacement should do that job at least as well.

## The closest free replacement: Samsung Food

Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) is the nearest match to Mealime's free experience. The free version lets you plan your weekly meals, turn them into a shopping list and share it with family. It can also send the list to grocery delivery from a set of retailers.

Samsung Food+ costs $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year and adds AI meal plans tailored to health goals, nutrition tracking and pantry management. If you only used Mealime for recipes and a list, the free version is probably enough.

## Free planning from your own recipes: Prepear

Prepear lets you save and import recipes with no limit, plan a full week and generate a grocery list sorted by category, all free. Prepear Gold costs $9.99 a month; its App Store listing shows other Gold plans from $59.99 to $119.99.

## Dinners chosen for you: eMeals

eMeals sends 7 new recipes a week from 15 meal and diet plans and turns your picks into a shopping list that can go straight to a grocery delivery or pickup partner. Twelve months costs $59.99 for dinners or $99.99 for all meals.

## Your own recipes on a calendar: Plan to Eat and Paprika

If you cooked a set of favorites in Mealime, these let you bring your own. Plan to Eat imports recipes from the web, schedules them and builds the shopping list, for $54.99 a year after a 14-day free trial. Paprika does the same with an aisle-sorted list and pantry tracking for $4.99 on iPhone, paid once, with an optional Premium in-app purchase. For a shared household list with a meal plan everyone can see, AnyList Complete costs $9.99 a year, or $14.99 for a household.

## For plans built to a calorie target: Eat This Much

Eat This Much builds meal plans automatically from your calorie and macro goals, the foods you like, your budget and your schedule. The free tier plans one day at a time. Premium, at $14.99 a month or $59.99 a year, plans the whole week and emails you a grocery list. There's a 14-day free trial.

It doesn't plan workouts, and families with different targets aren't individually optimized, according to its help pages.

## For macro targets without the logging: Prospre

Prospre plans your week to any calorie, protein, fat and carb target from foods you like, with a grocery list. Most of its yearly plans on the App Store are $49.99.

## For macro-focused plans with logging: Fitia and MyFitnessPal Premium+

Fitia generates meal plans around calorie and macro targets for weight loss or muscle gain, with an automatic shopping list and photo, voice and barcode logging in Premium. The App Store lists $19.99 for a month and $59.99 for a year.

MyFitnessPal added a meal planner, but only in its top tier, Premium+, at $24.99 a month or $99.99 a year. That makes sense if you already log food in MyFitnessPal and want planning in the same app.

## For meals and workouts in one plan: ZivaAI

ZivaAI plans a full week from a few questions: every meal with its recipe, calories and macros, workouts of 20 to 60 minutes for your equipment, and one grocery list sorted by aisle. You change anything by asking Ziva, the in-app coach, such as "make Thursday's dinner vegetarian," and the grocery list updates with it. You can also log what you ate, from a photo or a sentence.

## Which one should you pick?

- **You want Mealime back, free:** Samsung Food, or Prepear if you mostly cook saved recipes.
- **You want someone else to pick the week's dinners:** eMeals.
- **You cook your own recipes:** Plan to Eat or Paprika.
- **You eat to a calorie or protein target:** Eat This Much or Prospre, or Fitia if you also want to log what you eat.
- **You already use MyFitnessPal:** Premium+ adds the planner where your logs already are.
- **You want meals, workouts and groceries planned together:** ZivaAI.
